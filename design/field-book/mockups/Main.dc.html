On the rack

Webbing strap = the one thing to do next, one per screen. Bar-tack stitch on the cut end. Everything else is an index line.
Chalk ticks on the hold under your finger. Solid ring, broken ring, cross: readable without colour. The word is climbing slang for what you're about to grab.
Stamina halo rides above the helmet, so your eyes never leave the climber. Ten segments burn out while a limb is off the wall.

Tally scrap. Food and water are counted the way you'd count them in a notebook: strokes, crossed in fives. Taped to the corner of the screen.
Gear rack. The quick bar is a sling across the bottom of the screen. Each item hangs off a carabiner with a paper count tag.
Ink stamps for things that are final: a grade, a summit, a fall. Rough edges and missing ink come from a noise filter, so no two look clean.
